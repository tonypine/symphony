ActionWaiting on you · 20 minHuman Review

Symphony can't upload the TestFlight build for NOTE-88 without an App Store Connect API key. Nothing else in notes-app is blocked.

Stepsabout 10 min
  1. In App Store Connect, open Users and Access → Integrations → App Store Connect API.
