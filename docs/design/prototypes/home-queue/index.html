Queue

Symphony 0.0.1.1062

Try it

  • Drag a queue row onto another to reorder it.
  • Drag a row onto a slot, or press Start, to put it in front.
  • Change the number of slots with − and +.
  • Stop on a card frees the slot; the queue fills it.
  • Scroll the queue: the slots fold into a strip that stays on top.

New for the Gallery

  • Slot card (long), its empty slot, and its folded strip row
  • Phase track and checks bar on the card
  • Slot count control
  • Queue row with its number and grip

Prototype only

  • The first 20 rows are today's real tickets; the other 1,223 are generated to show scale.
