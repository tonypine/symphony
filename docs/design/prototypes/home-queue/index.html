Queue

Symphony 0.0.1.1062

Try it

  • Drag a queue row onto another to reorder it.
  • Drag a row onto a slot, or press Start, to put it in front.
  • Change the number of slots with − and +.
  • Stop on a card frees the slot; the queue fills it.
  • Scroll the queue: the slots fold into a strip that stays on top.
  • Click an epic’s row to show or hide its sub-tickets; drag it and they move with it.
  • Click an epic’s band to see what is still to come; click a “done” count, on the band or in the queue, to see what it has finished.
  • Drag a sub-ticket above one it depends on, or press Start on it: it is refused, with the reason.
